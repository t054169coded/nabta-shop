Three things that kill houseplants in Kuwait

01

The air-conditioning vent

Constant cold draught dries leaf edges brown within weeks. Keep every plant at least a metre from a vent.

02

Too much kindness

Overwatering kills more plants than drought. Push a finger in — if the soil is damp, wait another day.

03

Summer glass

From June to August, direct light through a window can scorch leaves. Pull plants back half a metre or diffuse it.
